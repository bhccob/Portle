# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: retail investors with positions across one or more brokers who want one private place for holdings and the reasoning behind them. First release is open sign-up; a public launch follows.
Action: open an archive (email sign-up, working form leading into /sign-up).
Proof on hand: none beyond the product itself; demo content is labelled "Sample". No testimonials, numbers or partner claims.
User steer (binding): keep it simple and calm so beginners aren't intimidated, while still feeling serious. Chosen from round one after two re-rolls; the user later explored lockers (glass lockers was built and shipped), then asked to return to the first vitrine designs as a combination of the first (headline left, thesis card, drawer row) and second (centred case, headline in the glass) comps, and picked blend A.
Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/blend/blend-a.png (supersedes .impeccable/mocks/ideas/glass-lockers.png and .impeccable/mocks/vitrine-close.png).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every position is a specimen kept under glass with the reasoning that put it there. One lit glass vitrine holding a cut amethyst geode whose growth bands are the thesis revisions, with its thesis card standing beside it, on a plinth above a row of glass drawers. Refuses the category default: phone mockup plus a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #171320 cabinet ground deepening into Amethyst #49366B; Lilac #9B82D7 and Lavender #D5C6F2 light the crystal and the labels; Porcelain #F3EFF8 text. Frosted vitrine glass with bright hairline edges; depth only from overlapping panes (no drop shadows); one integer depth scale drives blur, brightness and drift. One accession-label component (PF-#### · SYMBOL · status) repeats at every scale, hero to chip. Geometric sans with tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor understands that Porfael keeps the reasoning behind each position (thesis, bull/bear, invalidation, revisions) privately; believes it is calm and serious rather than hype; enters an email and opens an archive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav row: faceted P mark and wordmark left, Portfolio · Theses · Paper Lab · Guilds centred, Sign in right. A large clear glass vitrine fills the centre; the two-line headline "Keep the reasoning behind every position." is etched in its front glass. Inside, the glowing amethyst geode with leader-line labels Rev 1 · Mar 2025, Rev 2, Rev 3, Rev 4 · now, and the frosted thesis card "PF-0042 · ASML · Sample thesis" (Active, bull case, invalidation, Allocation 8.2%, Value Private). Along the bottom edge, five glass drawers link to Portfolio, Theses, Paper Lab, Guilds, Relics.
