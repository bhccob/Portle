# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: retail investors with positions across one or more brokers who want one private place for holdings and the reasoning behind them. First release is open sign-up; a public launch follows.
Action: open an archive (email sign-up, working form leading into /sign-up).
Proof on hand: none beyond the product itself; demo content is labelled "Sample". No testimonials, numbers or partner claims.
User steer (binding): keep it simple and calm so beginners aren't intimidated, while still feeling serious. Chosen from round one after two re-rolls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every position is a specimen kept under glass with the reasoning that put it there. One lit vitrine holds an amethyst geode whose growth bands are a thesis's revisions. Refuses the category default: phone mockup plus a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #171320 cabinet ground deepening into Amethyst #49366B; Lilac #9B82D7 and Lavender #D5C6F2 light the crystal and the labels; Porcelain #F3EFF8 text. Frosted vitrine glass with bright hairline edges; depth only from overlapping panes (no drop shadows); one integer depth scale drives blur, brightness and drift. One accession-label component (PF-#### · SYMBOL · status) repeats at every scale, hero to chip. Geometric sans with tabular numerals.
